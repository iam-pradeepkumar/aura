The problem · India & Nepal

Recent disasters expose a deadly gap

South Asia faces earthquakes, landslides, and floods every year — yet most communities lack affordable local warning and post-disaster survivor detection.

Jul 2024 · Kerala

Wayanad landslides

400+ deaths in the Western Ghats. Roads cut, villages buried. No hyper-local alert mesh or survivor sensing inside debris zones.

Oct 2023 · Sikkim

Glacial lake outburst flood

Teesta River flash flood killed 40+ and destroyed the Chungthang dam. Remote valleys had no last-mile evacuation routing.

Jan 2023 · Uttarakhand

Joshimath land subsidence

600+ buildings cracked in a pilgrimage town. Slow-motion disaster needed continuous monitoring — not one national bulletin.

Dec 2023 · Tamil Nadu

Chennai & coastal floods

Cyclone Michaung left streets underwater. Families needed ward-level safe-house maps on phones already on community WiFi.

Nov 2023 · Nepal

Jajarkot earthquake

Magnitude 6.4 struck western Nepal — 150+ killed, thousands homeless in remote hills. No village-level early warning or survivor sensing under collapsed homes.

Phase 1 — Early warning

Single ESP32 polls USGS + Open-Meteo. Hazards queue in DM Console. After approval: LAN multicast + webhook to phones.

Phase 2 — Rescue sensing

When networks fail, AURA_HUB hotspot links 4 RX + 1 TX ESP32. Laptop runs CSI pipeline: count, position, vitals.
