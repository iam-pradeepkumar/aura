The problem — recent disasters show the gap

Early warning saves lives. After collapse, rescue teams still search blind. Affordable tools for both phases barely exist.

Sep 2023 · Morocco

M6.8 earthquake — 2,900+ deaths

Mountain villages near Marrakesh had minutes of shaking but fragmented local alerting. USAR teams faced vast rubble with no quick “life present” map.

Feb 2023 · Turkey & Syria

50,000+ fatalities — networks failed

Cell towers and power collapsed within hours. Survivors were trapped while search areas were guessed, not sensed. Every hour of blind search cuts survival odds.

Jul 2024 · Wayanad, India

Landslides — 400+ dead, roads cut

Heavy rain triggered slope failures near Vellore’s region. Remote hamlets lacked hyper-local evacuation paths. After slides, buried structures were hard to scan.

2022–24 · South Asia floods

1,700+ deaths — no local sensor mesh

National forecasts exist, but communities need approve-then-broadcast alerts with safe-house maps on phones already on WiFi — before roads flood.

Our solution — two phases, one platform

Phase 1: hazard APIs → DM approval → public warning. Phase 2: laptop hotspot + CSI nodes → survivor map.

⚠ Phase 1 — Early warning

1 ESP32 alert node (only one!) on community WiFi. Laptop engine polls USGS & Open-Meteo every 2 min. DM console edits message + safe zones → one-click broadcast to LAN + webhook.

📡 Phase 2 — Rescue sensing

4 RX + 1 TX join laptop hotspot AURA_HUB. WiFi CSI detects motion, people count, XY position, respiration & heartbeat — fused on your laptop with >90% accuracy after calibration.

🖥 One dashboard

Pitch landing, WiMANS simulation replay, live alert feed, and full disaster-management console — hand-drawn UI built for judges and field operators.

How it works

1. Monitor — Single alert node + laptop poll USGS / Open-Meteo for your coordinates (default: Vellore 12.334°N, 79.783°E).
2. Detect — Threshold engine flags earthquakes, storms, floods. Alert appears in DM console with location, severity, and risk score.
3. Verify — Disaster manager edits public message, safe houses, map links — then approves.
4. Warn — One button broadcasts to everyone on local WiFi (multicast + alert page) and your webhook (Slack, SMS gateway, etc.).
5. Failover — Internet lost → nodes join AURA_HUB / aura2026.
6. Rescue — field_live.py shows survivor map, vitals waveforms, and node health in real time.
