The problem

Rescuers search blind in rubble, mud, and rain

No visibility

Thermal cameras and optics fail inside collapsed structures. Dogs and microphones cannot scale across city blocks.

Slow coverage

Human teams cannot systematically sweep large disaster polygons before the golden rescue window closes.

Alert gaps

Communities lack hyper-local early warning; after impact, coordination fragments across agencies.

Our solution

CSI sensing fleet + command dashboard

Mobile ESP32 nodes on spiderbots capture WiFi reflections from survivors. Drones relay coverage; the command center fuses count, position, respiration, and triage hints on a live map.

  • Mark a real disaster polygon on satellite imagery
  • Place survivor positions for simulation validation
  • Deploy spiderbots and drones — they patrol, home on CSI, and lock FOUND pins
  • Scale to Gazebo ROS 2 and NVIDIA Isaac Sim for photoreal training
